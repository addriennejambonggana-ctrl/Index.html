About Me

I am Addrienne Greg Jambonggana, a student who loves animation and multimedia arts. I enjoy turning stories into pictures, from rough storyboard sketches to finished visuals. I am also learning web design so I can present my creative work online. My goal is to grow into a skilled animator and multimedia artist who tells clear, memorable stories.
